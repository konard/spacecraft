# spacecraft
Concept of an autonomous spacecraft: a vehicle or base that captures all the energy and matter that pass through it and gives nothing away that it can reuse.

## Vision

Two closed systems share one hull:

1. **Energy** — every watt of sunlight, waste heat, laser input, or reentry heat is stored electrically or thermally before it is radiated away.
2. **Matter** — every atom of carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, and nitrogen that passes through the crew, the reactors, or the engines is caught and turned back into fuel, oxygen, water, or fertilizer.

The same chemistry that keeps a crew alive also balances a solar grid on Earth, fills a rocket on Mars, and floats a habitat on Venus. The documents below describe both halves and how they connect.
